Find the right massage for your body

Not sure which to choose? Tell us what's bothering you when you book and we'll tailor the session.

  • Therapeutic massage

    Focused treatment for muscle tension, stiffness and chronic pain, adjusted to your history and goals.

  • Deep tissue

    Slow, firm pressure that reaches deeper layers of muscle and fascia to release long-held knots.

  • Sports & rehabilitation

    Pre- and post-event work and injury rehab, informed by years with athletes and sports teams.

  • Swedish & relaxation

    Long, flowing strokes that calm the nervous system, ease stress and improve circulation.

  • Hot & cold stone

    Heated and chilled stones warm muscles, reduce inflammation and deepen relaxation.

  • Prenatal & postnatal

    Gentle, well-supported massage for comfort through pregnancy and recovery after birth.

  • Bamboo massage

    Warm bamboo tools roll and knead tissue for a deeply relaxing, even pressure.

  • Reflexology

    Targeted pressure on points in the feet to boost circulation and support whole-body balance.

  • Cupping (vacuum)

    Cups create gentle negative pressure on trigger points, lifting tissue and drawing in blood flow.

  • Tiger's point

    Precise trigger-point technique to release tight bands of muscle and referred pain.

About the studio

Clinical experience, delivered with a calm touch.

Massage Fenix is a small, independent studio in McKenzie Towne. Your therapist is a certified physiotherapist with more than three decades of experience in sports centres, hospitals, rehabilitation clinics, professional sports teams and spas.

That background means every session starts with understanding why you're sore, then choosing the techniques that will actually help, whether that's recovering from an injury, managing chronic tension or simply switching off for an hour.

  • Certified physiotherapist
  • 30+ years hands-on experience
  • Sessions tailored to your needs
