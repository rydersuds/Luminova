About Felix Etin, RMT

Clinical experience, delivered with a calm touch.

Massage Fenix is a small, independent studio in McKenzie Towne. Felix brings more than three decades of hands-on experience from sports centres, hospitals, rehabilitation clinics, professional sports teams and spas.

That background means every session starts with understanding why you're sore, then choosing the techniques that will actually help, whether that's recovering from an injury, managing chronic tension or simply switching off for an hour.
